# Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used by its owner alone, through rclone, to upload files
from the owner's Raspberry Pi into the owner's own Google Drive.

- The only Google account that authorises the client is the owner's.
- The client reads and writes files only inside the owner's Google Drive.
- No data is collected from anyone else, and no data is shared with,
  sold to, or stored by any third party.
- Access tokens are kept in the rclone configuration on the owner's own
  hardware and nowhere else.
- Revoking access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions ends all use
  immediately.

Questions can be raised as an issue on this repository.
